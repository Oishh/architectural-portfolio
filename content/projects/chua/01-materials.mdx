This room has three voices — the stone wall, the wood ceiling, and the floor. The rule we kept: only one of them raises its voice at a time. The stone gets to sing here; the ceiling hums; the floor is almost silent.

Material hierarchy is not a mood board. It's a decision about which surface you want someone's eye to land on when they forget why they walked in.
