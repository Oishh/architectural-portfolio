Bright rooms are easy. Rooms that know when to be dim — that's the craft. We built this one in three layers: a low ambient wash from the cove, pools of warm light where people actually sit, and small accents on the things worth looking at.

<PullQuote>
  The switch is a design element.
</PullQuote>

At dusk the ceiling fades and the room quietly reorganizes itself around the lamps. That's the point.
