A formal room fails when it forgets it is still a room people sit in. The millwork and the symmetry set a tone; the low lamp, the soft rug, and the one slightly asymmetrical pillow make it inhabitable.

<PullQuote>
  Formality is a frame, not a command.
</PullQuote>

You want the room to feel like it's dressed for an occasion, not like it's guarding against one.
