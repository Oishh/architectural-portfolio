Wall panels are often a decorative afterthought — ornament stuck onto a blank wall to make it feel considered. That's not what they do here. The panels set the rhythm of the room. Their module governs where the bed sits, where the sconces land, how the door is framed.

When the panel spacing is right, everything in the room agrees with it. When it's wrong, the room feels off even though you can't say why.
