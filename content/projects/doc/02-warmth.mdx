Warmth in a room is not a single thing. It's wool over wood over lamp-light over the faint reflection of the window. Peel any one layer off and the whole thing cools.

We spent a long afternoon adjusting just the undercoat color on the wood — not because anyone would notice the color alone, but because the temperature of everything above it was anchored to that one shade.
