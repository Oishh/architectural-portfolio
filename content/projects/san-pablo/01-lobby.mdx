Every patient walks in asking the same silent question — *am I in the right place, and will someone help me?* The lobby is the first answer.

We sized it for two kinds of waiting: the alone kind, where a person wants a quiet corner and a view of the door, and the together kind, where a family needs to sit close enough to hold a hand. The desk is high enough to feel staffed, low enough to feel human.
