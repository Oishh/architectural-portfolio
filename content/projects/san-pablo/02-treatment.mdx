Clinical does not have to mean cold. The rooms that heal best are calm — warm enough that a tired person does not feel like a specimen, clean enough that the staff can work at the speed their day demands.

We swapped fluorescent for indirect LED, carpet for a matte tile that looks like stone, and stainless-steel trim for PVC wood panels. Everything still cleans in under two minutes. Calm is a specification.
