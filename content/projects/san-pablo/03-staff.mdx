Patients spend minutes in a room. Staff spend years. We design clinics like waiting rooms, but the people who actually live in them are the nurses, the techs, the desk team who see this floor every day.

Sight lines from the station to every door. A break room with an actual window. Storage where the work happens, not three rooms away. If the staff is at ease, the patient is at ease — not the other way around.
