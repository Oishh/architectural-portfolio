Most rooms forget the ceiling. Four walls and a floor, and then whatever remains is painted flat. Here the roof is the room — it defines the volume more than any wall does.

The slope tilts light across the space through the day. In the morning it catches the east windows and sends a long stripe across the floor; by afternoon the stripe has moved and the wood has warmed half a shade. You design the day, not just the space.
