White walls are not the absence of decision. They are a commitment to let the light do the work — to let mornings and afternoons paint the room differently, to let a single plant throw a real shadow.

The mistake is thinking white means neutral. This white is warm, and the one in the bedroom is cooler, and the one on the ceiling is slightly yellower to sink under lamplight. Three whites. One looks simple.
