A bedroom is the last room of the day. Whatever you look at before you close your eyes is what your mind keeps turning over. That's an argument for restraint.

<PullQuote>
  A bedroom should end the sentence the day started.
</PullQuote>

Nothing on the walls that asks to be read. Nothing bright enough to pull you back from sleep. The one lamp is warm and dimmable and placed where your hand naturally lands.
