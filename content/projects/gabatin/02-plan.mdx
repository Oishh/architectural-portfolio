The living area wasn't sized for furniture. It was sized for the way a family gathers in the late afternoon — the hour when everyone drifts in from different parts of the house and ends up in the same room without deciding to.

<PullQuote>
  If you have to decorate, the plan didn't finish its job.
</PullQuote>

Everything else — the finishes, the lighting, the art — is restraint on top of a plan that already worked. That's the order. Plan first. Restraint second. Decoration never.
