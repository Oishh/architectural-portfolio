The front of a house is a promise. Before anyone steps inside, the facade has already told them what to expect — how warm, how private, how heavy.

We spent an unreasonable amount of time on the concrete louver blocks. They had to read as solid at a distance and dissolve as you walked up. Not a trick; a gift. The moment you see through the weave, you're already on the porch.
